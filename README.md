---

I build the kind of systems that should stay out of the way.

Most of my time sits between **networks** and **automation** — making packets go where they should, then making machines handle the rest.

If something is repetitive, fragile, or annoying to do by hand, I usually try to take myself out of the loop.

---

## What I Do

- 🌐 **Networks** — routing, tunnels, DNS, firewalls. I care about paths that stay predictable.
- ⚙️ **Automation** — bots, agents, and small services that keep running after I close the laptop.
- 🧠 **LLM systems** — not chat for chat's sake. Wiring models into real workflows, with fallbacks when they fail.
- 🖥️ **Self-hosted infra** — boxes, containers, reverse proxies, the boring layer that everything else sits on.
- 🔒 **Access & ops** — least surprise, least noise. If it needs a dashboard every day, the design is probably wrong.

---

## How I Think

I don't ship things to look busy.

A problem shows up, it wastes time, I automate it. The best work is the piece I forget is still running.

Quiet infrastructure beats clever infrastructure.
